Welcome to MyICE ⚕️

MyICE stands for My In Case of Emergency. It is your personal health vault — a safe place to store everything a doctor, paramedic, or family member would need to know about you in an emergency, or just to help manage your health every day.

You do not need to fill everything in at once. Start with the most important things — your medications, allergies, and emergency contacts — and add more over time.

🚨 For First Responders — The Emergency Card

This is the most important feature in MyICE. If you are ever in an accident, unconscious, or unable to speak, a paramedic or emergency room doctor can pull up your emergency information instantly — no login, no password, no app download needed.

Your emergency page is always available at:
keiko-dev-lcai.github.io/myice

A first responder simply opens that link on any phone or computer and taps Emergency View at the top of the screen. They will immediately see your:

  • Blood type and critical medical alerts
  • Current medications and dosages
  • Allergies (especially drug allergies)
  • Emergency contacts and their phone numbers
  • DNR / advance directive status
  • Primary doctor name and phone number

Tip: Write the URL on a card in your wallet or put it on your phone's lock screen. That way anyone who finds you knows exactly where to look.

👨‍👩‍👧 Caregiver & Family Access — Why It Matters

Many people rely on a family member, spouse, or professional caregiver to help manage their health. MyICE makes this safe and simple.

You can set up a Caregiver PIN — a separate access code that lets a trusted person add notes, update records, and view your information, but not change your critical settings or delete your data. The Owner PIN (your full access code) stays private to you.

You can also add Family Profiles — meaning one person in a family can manage health records for a spouse, parent, or child all in one place. Each profile is kept separate and private.

Why this matters: If you are ever in the hospital and cannot speak for yourself, a family member with your caregiver access can quickly show doctors your full medical history, current medications, and advance directives. This can save critical time.

🌍 Access Your Records Anywhere in the World

MyICE is a web app — meaning you can open it on any phone, tablet, or computer, anywhere in the world, just by going to:
keiko-dev-lcai.github.io/myice

Free users store data in their browser. This means your records are on that specific device. If you switch phones or clear your browser, the data stays behind.

Premium subscribers ($3/month in LCAI) get their data backed up to the blockchain — meaning you can log in from any device, anywhere, and your full health records are always there. If you lose your phone, travel overseas, or visit a specialist in another city, everything is still accessible.

Install to your home screen: MyICE works like a real app on your phone. On iPhone, open it in Safari and tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen." On Android, tap the menu and tap "Add to Home Screen." It will look and feel just like a regular app — no App Store needed.

🤖 AI Health Assistant

MyICE includes an AI health assistant powered by Lightchain AI — a decentralized AI network. The AI is available to Premium subscribers and can help you:

  • Understand what a medication is used for and how it works
  • Check whether your medications might interact with each other
  • Understand your lab results in plain English
  • Analyze patterns in your symptom journal
  • Generate questions to ask your doctor before an appointment
  • Read and summarize uploaded medical documents

Important: The AI provides general background information only. It is not a doctor and does not give medical advice. Always check with your doctor or pharmacist before changing anything about your health care. AI responses may take up to 2 minutes — this is normal.

🔐 Your Privacy & How Data Is Stored

Free users: All your data is stored only on your device, in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. Nobody can see your records except you (and anyone you give your PIN to). The downside is that if you clear your browser or switch devices, the data does not move with you.

Premium users: Your emergency data is backed up to the Lightchain blockchain. Your private health records are encrypted before they are stored — meaning only someone with your password can read them. Not even the app can read your private data without your password.

MyICE does not sell your data, show you ads, or share anything with third parties. Your health information is yours.

✅ Quick Start — What to Do First
  1. Go to My Profile and enter your name, date of birth, and blood type
  2. Go to Medical Alerts and add your most critical conditions (e.g. diabetic, heart condition)
  3. Go to Medications and add everything you take, including over-the-counter meds
  4. Go to Allergies and add any drug or food allergies — especially life-threatening ones
  5. Go to Emergency Contacts and add at least one person with a phone number
  6. Install MyICE to your phone home screen so it is always one tap away
  7. Tell a family member or caregiver about the emergency URL so they know where to find your information

That is it — you now have a working emergency medical ID. Everything else you can fill in over time.

💡 Help & FAQ
What is MyICE?

MyICE ⚕️ is your personal health vault and emergency medical ID. ICE stands for In Case of Emergency — a universal standard recognized by paramedics, police, and ER doctors worldwide.


You store your health information once, and it is available instantly — whether you need to share it with a new doctor, hand your phone to a paramedic, or look up a medication while traveling.

Is my data private?

Yes. All health data is stored on your device only — in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your phone or computer unless you explicitly share it. We have no server storing your records. No one at MyICE can see your data.


The only time data leaves your device is when you use an AI feature (like Lab Analysis or Drug Interaction Check). In that case, only the specific text you submit is sent to our AI server for processing — and it is discarded immediately after. No personal information is retained on our servers.

What is the Emergency tab?

The Emergency tab is the first button on the screen — a large red button that anyone can tap without a PIN or login. It shows only the information you have chosen to make emergency-visible, such as your blood type, allergies, current medications, emergency contacts, and DNR status.


How to set it up: In each section (Allergies, Medications, Conditions, etc.), look for the "Show in Emergency" toggle on each entry. Turn it on for anything you want paramedics or ER staff to see instantly.


You control exactly what is visible. Everything else stays completely private.

How do Medical Alerts work?

Medical Alerts are bold warning banners shown at the very top of the app — visible to anyone who opens it, before they even tap Emergency. Think of them as a digital version of a medical alert bracelet.


Examples: ⚠️ DIABETIC · ⚠️ DO NOT RESUSCITATE · ⚠️ SEVERE PENICILLIN ALLERGY · ⚠️ ALZHEIMER'S PATIENT


Go to the Medical Alerts section in the sidebar to add, edit, or remove alerts. You decide what is shown and what stays private.

What is the PIN system?

MyICE has two PIN levels to protect your records:


Owner PIN — Full access. Add, edit, and delete anything. Set this in Settings.


Caregiver PIN — Limited access. A family member or caregiver can view records and add new entries, but cannot edit or delete existing records. Set this in Settings.


Emergency View — No PIN needed. Anyone can tap Emergency and see only what you marked as emergency-visible. They cannot access anything else in the app.


If you do not set a PIN, the app is fully open. We recommend setting at least an Owner PIN to protect your private health records.

What are the free vs. paid AI features?

Free — always included:

  • Emergency tab and Medical Alerts
  • All health record storage (conditions, medications, allergies, contacts, history, vitals, appointments, billing, and more)
  • Vitals graphs and tracking
  • Symptom journal
  • Doctor questions notepad
  • Family profiles

Premium AI features — $3/month in LCAI:

  • Lab result analysis — plain-English explanation of your blood work
  • Drug interaction checker — AI scans your full medication list for conflicts
  • Medication explainer — what each drug does, side effects, warnings
  • Document AI — upload a medical document, AI reads and files it
  • Symptom pattern analysis — AI spots trends in your journal
  • Appointment question suggester — personalized questions for your next visit
  • AI health assistant chat
How do I pay for Premium?

Premium is billed monthly in LCAI — the native token of the Lightchain AI network. Payment is made directly from your crypto wallet (Trust Wallet or any WalletConnect-compatible wallet).


To subscribe, go to Settings and tap Upgrade to Premium. You will be prompted to send the current monthly amount in LCAI to activate your subscription.


LCAI can be purchased on major crypto exchanges and DEXes. You can also bridge LCAI to Lightchain mainnet at bridge.lightchain.ai.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Because your data is stored in browser localStorage, clearing your browser's site data will erase your MyICE records. Always export a backup before clearing browser data.


To export your data, go to Settings → Export My Data. This creates a JSON backup file you can save to your device.


This is also why we recommend using MyICE on a device you own and trust — not a public or shared computer.

How do I add a family member's profile?

Go to Family Profiles in the sidebar and tap Add Profile. Give it a name (e.g. "Mom", "Dad", "My Child"). Each profile is fully separate — its own records, emergency info, and settings.


Switch between profiles using the profile selector at the top of the app. Each profile can have its own Owner and Caregiver PINs.

Is MyICE available on mobile?

Yes — MyICE is a web app that works on any device with a browser, including iPhone and Android. Open it in your phone's browser (Safari, Chrome, etc.) and add it to your home screen for instant one-tap access.


On iPhone: Tap the Share button in Safari → Add to Home Screen.

On Android: Tap the menu in Chrome → Add to Home Screen.


This puts a MyICE icon on your home screen that opens like an app — no app store download needed.
